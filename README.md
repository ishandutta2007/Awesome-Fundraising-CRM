# Awesome-Fundraising-CRM

## Top Fundraising CRM & Donor Management Platforms



A curated list of leading fundraising CRMs and donor management systems for nonprofits — covering constituent relationship management, donation tracking, online giving, campaign management, stewardship, reporting, and engagement tools.  

**Primary focus: open-source software.**



Commercial / hosted platforms are listed separately for completeness. Open-source alternatives and community tools are emphasized throughout.



---



## SaaS / Hosted Platforms



| Platform | Description | Key Focus |

|----------|-------------|-----------|

| **[Bloomerang](https://bloomerang.co/)** | Donor management CRM focused on retention, engagement scoring, constituent timelines, and easy-to-use fundraising tools for small to mid-sized nonprofits. | Donor retention & engagement |

| **[DonorPerfect](https://www.donorperfect.com/)** | Comprehensive donor management and fundraising software with robust reporting, marketing tools, gift tracking, and scalability for growing organizations. | Full-featured donor management |

| **[Blackbaud Raiser’s Edge NXT](https://www.blackbaud.com/)** | Enterprise-grade constituent and fundraising CRM with deep relationship data, AI insights, annual fund tools, and extensive nonprofit ecosystem integrations. | Enterprise fundraising CRM |

| **[Neon CRM](https://www.neoncrm.com/)** (Neon One) | Flexible nonprofit CRM combining donor management, online fundraising, events, memberships, and email tools with strong reporting and automation. | All-in-one nonprofit CRM |

| **[Kindful](https://www.kindful.com/)** | Nonprofit CRM with fundraising analytics, donation tracking, and marketing integrations aimed at growing organizations that want connected tools. | Fundraising CRM + integrations |

| **[Donorbox](https://donorbox.org/)** | Online fundraising platform with donation forms, peer-to-peer campaigns, events, and a lightweight CRM for managing donors and recurring gifts. | Online giving + simple CRM |

| **[Givebutter](https://www.givebutter.com/)** | Free/affordable all-in-one fundraising platform with donation forms, peer-to-peer, events, ticketing, and built-in CRM for unlimited contacts. | Free/affordable fundraising + CRM |

| **[Little Green Light](https://www.littlegreenlight.com/)** | Flexible, cloud-based donor management and fundraising platform popular with small-to-mid nonprofits for gift tracking, reporting, and customization. | Affordable donor management |

| **[EveryAction](https://www.bonterratech.com/)** (Bonterra) | Unified CRM and fundraising platform (formerly EveryAction) focused on engagement, automation, moves management, and multi-channel campaigns. | Engagement & multi-channel fundraising |

| **[Keela](https://www.keela.co/)** | Nonprofit CRM with smart donation asks, marketing automation, wealth screening insights, and tools designed to help teams raise more efficiently. | Smart fundraising & automation |

## Open-Source Softwares



Fundraising and nonprofit CRM has one clear, mature open-source leader (CiviCRM), plus related open packages and lighter tools. Fully featured commercial alternatives remain dominant for turnkey online giving and polished UX, but open-source options provide complete data ownership and no licensing fees.



### Core Frameworks & Nonprofit / Fundraising CRMs



| Project | Description | License | Notes |

|---------|-------------|---------|-------|

| **[CiviCRM](https://civicrm.org/)** | The leading open-source constituent relationship management system built specifically for nonprofits, NGOs, and advocacy organizations. Handles contacts, contributions, memberships, events, campaigns, cases, mailings, and reporting. Integrates with WordPress, Drupal, Joomla, and Backdrop. | AGPLv3 | Most complete open-source fundraising CRM |

| **[Salesforce Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP)](https://github.com/SalesforceFoundation/NPSP)** | Open-source managed package that turns Salesforce into a nonprofit CRM with fundraising, household, and program management data models. Runs on the Salesforce platform (free licenses available via Power of Us for eligible orgs). | BSD-3-Clause | Powerful but requires Salesforce platform |

| **Salesforce open nonprofit modules** | Additional open-source Salesforce.org packages such as Program Management Module and Outbound Funds Module for program tracking and grantmaking. | Open source | Extensions for Salesforce nonprofit stack |

| **Other open CRM foundations** | General open-source CRMs (e.g., SuiteCRM, EspoCRM, Twenty) that can be customized for donor management, though they lack native nonprofit fundraising features. | Various | Customizable bases for donor tracking |



### Specialized Libraries & Related Tools



| Project | Description | Focus Area |

|---------|-------------|---------|

| **Online giving & form tools** | Open-source form builders (Formbricks, HeyForm, etc.) and payment integrations that can power donation pages connected to CiviCRM or custom CRMs. | Donation forms |

| **Email & communication** | Open mailing tools and CiviCRM’s built-in CiviMail for newsletters, appeals, and stewardship. | Donor communication |

| **Reporting & analytics** | CiviCRM reports, plus Metabase, Apache Superset, or custom dashboards for fundraising metrics. | Fundraising analytics |

| **Payment gateway connectors** | Open integrations with Stripe, PayPal, and other processors commonly used with self-hosted CRMs. | Payment processing |

| **Membership & event modules** | Native CiviCRM components (CiviMember, CiviEvent) and related open extensions. | Memberships & events |

| **Data migration & ETL** | Open tools for importing legacy donor data into CiviCRM or other systems. | Data migration |



### Additional Notable Open-Source Tools



- **CiviCRM ecosystem** — Large community of extensions for accounting, payment processors, SMS, advanced fundraising workflows, and CMS integrations.

- **Self-hosted stacks** — CiviCRM + WordPress/Drupal + open form/payment tools for a complete low-cost fundraising system.

- **Salesforce NPSP route** — Eligible nonprofits can obtain free Salesforce licenses and install the open-source NPSP package for a highly capable (though platform-dependent) solution.

- **Custom donor databases** — Lighter open CRMs or even Airtable/Baserow-style tools extended with forms and payment webhooks for very small organizations.

- **Advocacy & engagement add-ons** — Open tools for petitions, email campaigns, and constituent engagement that complement core fundraising CRM functions.

- **Accounting bridges** — Connectors to open-source or affordable accounting systems for gift and revenue reconciliation.



**Note:** Commercial fundraising CRMs excel at polished online giving experiences, integrated payment processing, wealth screening, AI-driven insights, and turnkey support. CiviCRM is the strongest fully open-source alternative and is used by thousands of nonprofits worldwide; it requires more technical setup and maintenance than pure SaaS but offers unmatched flexibility and zero licensing cost. Salesforce NPSP provides another powerful open-source data model for organizations already in or willing to adopt the Salesforce ecosystem.
